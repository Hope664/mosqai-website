How it works

Beyond the capture, in four quiet steps.

01

Lure

Soft UV-A light rings the entry holes while a CO₂ source mimics human breath. Mosquitoes come looking.

02

Capture

A quiet fan pulls them down into a sealed, opaque chamber. Nothing gets out, and nobody has to look at them.

03

Snap

An angled camera photographs the catch and sends the images over Wi-Fi from the built-in ESP32 board.

04

Know

Our AI counts and identifies each mosquito. You see the results and the risk level in the MosqAI app.
